Private intelligence systems

Galidima is a local-first system I use to study private inference, bounded context, and verifiable evidence.

The model is not treated as a source of record. Operational data, calculations, and verification state remain on explicit paths. Au Jour Le Jour enters through a read-only finance contract; MyCasaPro remains separate until a narrow, verified integration contract exists.
