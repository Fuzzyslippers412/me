Au Jour Le Jour

Local-first Household ledger Live
Visit Au Jour Le Jour Contact Projects

Au Jour Le Jour is a live local-first household essentials ledger. It tracks recurring bills, links household use across devices without logins, and stays intentionally tracker-only: no bank connections and no payment execution.

What It Solves

  • Keeps recurring essentials in one local-first ledger instead of bank-linked automation or scattered notes.
  • Lets a household link multiple devices without accounts, with invite, recovery, and per-member contribution tracking.
  • Adds an available-now planner, system overview, and monthly/yearly progress views for deciding what to cover first.
  • Supports JSON/CSV export, yearly receipt PDF export, backups, and read-only sharing.
  • Keeps a serious local runtime: Mamdou quick commands, Janitor audits, and explicit integration endpoints for MyCasaPro.

Status

Live at aujourlejour.xyz. The current build includes linked-household flows, mobile install and handoff, allocation planning, progress tracking with prepaid-month handling, receipt export, Mamdou quick commands, and Janitor diagnostics.
