Project

Au Jour Le Jour

Local data No money movement Live
Visit the site Projects

Private recurring-bill and essentials tracker with monthly planning, due-state views, readable backups, and optional household sharing. It never connects to a bank or moves money.

Use

Recurring templates become monthly items with pending, partial, completed, or skipped state.

The interface separates what needs attention, shows what remains for the month, and keeps reserved funds distinct from ordinary bills.

Exports include CSV, JSON, yearly receipt PDFs, and a readable HTML backup that also retains the importable recovery data.

Boundaries

The application records and plans. It does not connect to a bank, execute payments, or claim to know the balance of an account.

Sharing is optional and read-only; the local build remains available with SQLite storage and explicit diagnostic tooling.

Current state

Live at aujourlejour.xyz. The published build includes monthly planning, due-state views, read-only sharing, export, and recovery; the local build adds SQLite storage, quick commands, and Janitor checks.
