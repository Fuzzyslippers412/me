Au Jour Le Jour

Local data No money movement Live

Project by Armel Tenkiang.

Visit the site Projects

Au Jour Le Jour is a live, private tracker for recurring and essential bills. It plans and records; it does not connect to a bank or move money.

Ledger

  • Recurring templates produce monthly items with pending, partial, completed, or skipped state.
  • The action queue separates overdue items, the next seven days, and later work; monthly and annual views track progress and reserved funds.
  • English, French, German, and Portuguese run across the web and local editions. Interface language remains separate from locale and currency formatting.
  • Exports include JSON, CSV, annual PDF receipts, readable HTML backups, and an aggregate year-review PNG that omits bill-level details.

Runtime boundaries

  • The static web edition stores its ledger in the current browser; the local edition uses SQLite and operates without a hosted dependency.
  • Read-only sharing uses a separate relay with privacy toggles, expiry, snapshot or live mode, and explicit publication.
  • The local Mamdou assistant supports Qwen OAuth, OpenAI, or Anthropic. Common commands take a deterministic fast path; every mutation still requires proposal and confirmation.
  • The annual finance contract uses integer-cent totals and two reconciliation identities. It returns a conflict instead of publishing inconsistent totals.
  • Janitor exercises functional, adversarial, property, hygiene, provider, relay, and degraded-runtime paths.

Galidima integration

Au Jour Le Jour exposes a read-only finance contract with integer-cent amounts, explicit coverage, and reconciliation identities. Galidima uses it for deterministic financial answers and retains snapshots with provenance and freshness.

Galidima technical note

Engineering frame

Constraint
The web edition keeps its ledger in browser storage; the local edition uses SQLite. The interface must state that difference instead of implying equal durability.
Boundary
Monthly states, integer-cent amounts, and frequent commands follow deterministic paths. Assistant-proposed mutations require explicit confirmation.
Recovery
Readable exports, SQLite backups, safe mode, and read-only sharing are separate paths. Janitor verifies contracts, relay behaviour, and degraded states.
