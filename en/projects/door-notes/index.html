From a question to a source

A reader can explore a profession or specify a role and workplace. Occupation matching retains ambiguity rather than forcing an arbitrary match.

Workplace relationships depend on the selected role. The history of a university does not automatically establish an appointment in every department.

Sharing without personal inputs

Public examples and shareable links use public inputs. Personal research entries remain in the reader’s tab.

Documented histories can be read without a saved personal trace; filters and maps add interaction without replacing the text and sources.

Engineering frame

Constraint
A workforce statistic, a historical episode and a workplace relationship do not establish the same kind of fact.
Decision
Records retain sources, dates, locators and limits. Research leads stay separate from documented relationships.
Limit
A matching name, job advertisement or grant does not by itself establish a hiring history.

Current state

Live at doornotes.org. This account is based on reviewed source and documentation, not an individual assessment of career opportunities.
