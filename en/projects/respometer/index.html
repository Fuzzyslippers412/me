Respometer

Current events Evidence Live

Project by Armel Tenkiang.

Visit Respometer Projects

Respometer is a live platform for structured current-event analysis. It starts with quick harm assessment from links, source text, or notes, then moves into multi-actor scoring, incident-linked analyses, evidence updates, revision history, comparison views, and export-ready public pages.

Use

  • Gives each event a structured harm assessment before the full actor ranking begins.
  • Keeps actors, evidence, confidence, and revisions together so a public analysis can evolve without losing its audit trail.
  • Supports same-incident linking and side-by-side comparison between related analyses.
  • Lets contributors stage evidence updates while editors decide what becomes part of the live reading.
  • Publishes public or unlisted result pages with share links, PNG/story cards, and PDF export.

Engineering frame

Constraint
A parseable page is not automatically sufficient evidence. Live blogs, opinion, explainers, and event reports have different evidentiary shapes.
Boundary
Source classification precedes extraction. Zod schemas, scoring functions, and RLS policies remain deterministic and separate from optional assisted intake.
Revision
Score audits and revision records preserve the path from sources to assessments and the public result when evidence or confidence changes.
