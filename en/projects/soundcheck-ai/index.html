Project

Soundcheck.AI

Financial filings Spending verification Active Updated Sep 1, 2026

Project by Armel Tenkiang.

Visit Soundcheck.AI Projects

Analyzes public nonprofit and school financial filings (including IRS records) to surface spending patterns and check public claims.

Use

  • Summarizes filings for quick review.
  • Flags spending patterns that diverge from public claims.
  • Keeps evidence trails for donors, boards, and auditors.

Design frame

Constraint
An extracted value is not a conclusion. Fiscal year, form, unit, and source must remain attached before reported and observed spending can be compared.
Boundary
Public filings remain the primary evidence. Analysis organizes entries and anomalies but does not replace the document or automatically turn a pattern into an allegation.
Review
Each check must resolve to its source page or line item, retain the applied transformation, and accept correction when the underlying filing changes.

Current State

Current work is on filing ingestion, normalization, and verification summaries.
