Programming update

Recurring game discovery and directory trust

July 28, 2026 Liga do Povo Verified change 18a66d6

The pickup directory was expanded around recurring community games, clearer participation rules, and more reliable regional discovery.

What changed

The directory gained additional field and recurring-series coverage, including Dallas and North Jersey, while keeping area preference explicit.

Community listings now distinguish Liga attendance, external registration, informational listings, known capacity, and public-location access instead of treating every game as the same kind of RSVP.

Recurring schedule, area preference, participation safety, and production persistence are covered by dedicated checks and database migrations.

Provenance
